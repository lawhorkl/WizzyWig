KB_BUILD_RECIPE:LLM_OPTIMIZED_ADAPTIVE_WOW_API_KB
==================================================
AUDIENCE:LLM_ONLY
GOAL:maximize_WoW_addon_codegen_debug_accuracy;minimize_token_load
SOURCE_DEFAULT:wow_reference/wow-ui-source
BUILD_CMD:node scripts/kb-build.mjs wow_reference/wow-ui-source .
DESIGN:source_enumerating;version_adaptive;diagnostic_driven

================================================================================
PRINCIPLES
================================================================================
1_EXACT_FACTS_FIRST:
  Generated_Blizzard_API_docs_are_authoritative_for_signatures.
  Extract_names,args,returns,nilability,defaults,enums,events,structures,flags.
  Do_not_rewrite_structured_facts_by_hand.

2_SOURCE_ENUMERATING_NOT_VERSION_HARDCODED:
  Enumerate_all_Interface/AddOns/**/*Documentation.lua_files.
  Parse_by_documentation_table_shape_not_by_known_file_list.
  Do_not_assume_retail,current_patch,specific_API_counts,or_specific_system_names.
  New_WoW_versions_should_add_records_automatically_when_docs_follow_known_shape.

3_LLM_OPTIMIZED_OUTPUT:
  Humans_are_not_the_reader.
  No_narrative_explanations.
  No_markdown_prose_sections.
  Use_dense_stable_records_with_exact_API_names.
  One_canonical_record_per_fact.
  Prefer_small_topic_shards_over_a_large_duplicated_master.

4_DIAGNOSTICS_OVER_SILENT_OMISSION:
  Parse_failures_unknown_fields_missing_required_symbols_go_to_kb/diagnostics.txt.
  Use_UNKNOWN_or_UNVERIFIED_markers_when_a_fact_is_not_exactly_extracted.
  Never_hide_unsupported_documentation_shapes.

5_TARGETED_AUDIT_ONLY:
  Audit_is_not_manual_rereading.
  Audit_reviews_diagnostics,required_symbols,high_risk_addon_patterns,and_token_efficiency.
  Add_GOTCHA_or_PATTERN_records_only_when_they_prevent_common_codegen_debug_errors.

================================================================================
PIPELINE
================================================================================
STEP:1_DISCOVER
  INPUT:WoW_UI_source_root(default:wow_reference/wow-ui-source)
  VERIFY:Interface/_exists
  VERSION:version.txt_or_UNKNOWN
  DOC_FILES:all_Interface/AddOns/**/*Documentation.lua

STEP:2_EXTRACT_GENERATED_DOCS
  SCRIPT:scripts/kb-extract-docs.mjs
  PARSE:Lua_table_subset_used_by_Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated
  RECORD:
    FN:C_Namespace.Function_or_GlobalFunction
    METHOD:ScriptObject:Method
    EVENT:LITERAL_EVENT_NAME
    ENUM:EnumName
    TYPE:StructureName
  FIELDS:
    LOC,PARAMS,RET,PAYLOAD,VALUES,FIELDS,FLAGS,DOC
  PRESERVE:
    nilability,default_values,inner_types,MayReturnNothing,protected_secret_flags
  DIAGNOSTICS:
    parse_failures,unknown_keys,parsed_file_count,total_doc_file_count

STEP:3_SCAN_SOURCE_GAPS
  SCRIPT:scripts/kb-scan-source.mjs
  PURPOSE:route_and_gotcha_records_for_concepts_not_fully_encoded_in_generated_docs
  TARGETS:
    ChatFrame_*,ChatTypeInfo,ChatTypeGroup,chat_filters
    ScrollingMessageFrameMixin
    CreateFrame,CreateFramePool,CreateFramePoolCollection
    Mixin,CreateFromMixins
    color_escape_|cAARRGGBB_|r,WrapTextInColorCode,ColorMixin
    hyperlink_|H_|h,SetHyperlink,ItemRef
    frame_templates,font_objects,XML_script_handlers
  OUTPUT:PATTERN_or_GOTCHA_records_only

STEP:4_WRITE_LLM_SHARDS
  SCRIPT:scripts/kb-build.mjs
  OUTPUTS:
    WOW_API_KB.txt:route_index_only
    kb/index.txt:topic_routes_counts_required_symbols
    kb/api_functions.txt:C_*_and_global_documented_functions
    kb/widgets_frames.txt:ScriptObject_methods_Frame_Button_EditBox_FontString_Region
    kb/events.txt:events_and_payloads
    kb/enums_types.txt:enums_structures_constants_tables
    kb/chat_comm.txt:chat_addon_message_chatframe_scrollingmessageframe_records
    kb/ui_patterns.txt:CreateFrame_Mixin_color_hyperlink_font_pattern_gotcha_records
    kb/diagnostics.txt:parse_validation_scan_audit_status

STEP:5_VALIDATE
  SCRIPT:scripts/kb-validate.mjs
  CHECK:
    all_expected_output_files_exist
    required_symbols_present
    no_duplicate_canonical_records_across_topic_files
    parse_failures_are_reported
    unknown_fields_are_reported
  REQUIRED_SYMBOLS:
    C_ChatInfo.RegisterAddonMessagePrefix
    C_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage
    CHAT_MSG_ADDON
    ChatFrame_MessageEventHandler
    ScrollingMessageFrame:AddMessage
    SimpleFontString:SetText
    SimpleEditBox:SetText
    SimpleButton:Click
    SimpleFrame:SetScript
    Region:SetPoint
    CreateFrame
    Mixin
    CreateFromMixins
    |cAARRGGBB
    |Htype:payload

STEP:6_TARGETED_AUDIT
  READ:kb/diagnostics.txt
  REVIEW_ONLY:
    PARSE_FAIL
    UNKNOWN_KEYS
    VALIDATION_FAIL
    high_risk_chat_comm_entries
    protected_secure_taint_sensitive_entries
    frame_widget_creation_entries
    hyperlink_color_font_entries
    topic_file_size_and_duplicate_prose
  ACTION:
    fix_parser_or_scanner_when_systematic
    add_compact_GOTCHA/PATTERN_when_source_behavior_matters_to_codegen
    record_UNVERIFIED_or_UNKNOWN_when_exact_fact_cannot_be_extracted
  PROHIBITED:
    broad_manual_summary
    prose_rewrite_of_generated_signatures
    duplicated_master_KB_content

================================================================================
RECORD_FORMAT
================================================================================
FN:ExactFunctionName
SYS:SystemName
LOC:relative/source/file.lua
PARAMS:name:type?,other:type=default
RET:name:type?|void|nothing
FLAGS:ProtectedOrSecretFlag=value
DOC:short_underscore_compacted_doc_when_useful

METHOD:ObjectType:MethodName
LOC:relative/source/file.lua
PARAMS:self,arg:type?
RET:value:type|void
FLAGS:...

EVENT:EVENT_NAME
SYS:SystemName
LOC:relative/source/file.lua
PAYLOAD:name:type?,other:type
FLAGS:...

ENUM:EnumName
LOC:relative/source/file.lua
VALUES:Name=0,Other=1
RANGE:n=2,min=0,max=1

TYPE:StructureName
KIND:Structure|CallbackType|Table
LOC:relative/source/file.lua
FIELDS:name:type?,other:table<InnerType>

PATTERN:Name
SIG:compact_signature_or_syntax
LOC:sample_source_locations

GOTCHA:Name
DESC:compact_codegen_warning

================================================================================
TOKEN_RULES
================================================================================
No_human_narrative.
No_repeated_canonical_records.
No_long_examples.
Examples_max_4_lines_only_when_they_prevent_common_errors.
Use_exact_names_for_grep.
Use_topic_shards_before_master.
Use_kb/index.txt_for_routing.
Use_kb/diagnostics.txt_before_trusting_unusual_or_changed_API_areas.

================================================================================
RECOVERY
================================================================================
IF_WOW_SOURCE_UPDATED:
  run_BUILD_CMD
  inspect_kb/diagnostics.txt
  perform_STEP:6_TARGETED_AUDIT

IF_PARSE_FAILS_IN_NEW_VERSION:
  inspect_failed_file_shape
  update_parser_to_support_shape_generically
  rerun_BUILD_CMD
  avoid_version_specific_special_cases_unless_shape_is_truly_version_specific

IF_REQUIRED_SYMBOL_MISSING:
  grep_source_for_symbol
  if_symbol_removed_in_version:record_version_gap_in_diagnostics
  if_symbol_exists:fix_extractor_or_scanner

================================================================================
END
